# Product

## Platform
Web, responsive mobile and desktop.

## Stack
Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, OpenAI SDK targeting Nebius Nemotron, official Tavily SDK.

## Users and purpose
Students finishing high school or attending university seeking international scholarships. Research real opportunities, understand deceptive offers, and prepare an application timeline. Global hackathon jury; UI in English. Mock workflow must work without any external APIs.

## Constraints
Explain trust and eligibility separately. Expose uncertainty and missing requirements. Do not portray fixture deadlines as verified calls. MIT license. User authorized implementation directly; no brand assets supplied.
